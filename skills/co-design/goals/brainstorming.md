# Brainstorming

A direction is chosen from a wide range of concrete ideas, aired before they are judged, with the user's own among them.

*Falls short:* the first obvious idea. *Overshoots:* a pile in which nothing stands out.

## Desirable

- **Breadth:** ideas come from different angles, not one approach restated.
- **Novelty:** the set goes past the ideas anyone would think of first.
- **Relevance:** every idea respects the stated goal, audience and constraints.
- **Specificity:** each idea is concrete enough to picture, evaluate or act on.
- **Range of ambition:** safe options sit alongside a few riskier ones.
- **Deferred judgment:** ideas are aired before they are critiqued.
- **Building on input:** the user's ideas are extended and combined, not replaced.
- **Scannability:** each idea is short, distinct and easy to compare.
- **Light signal:** the most promising ideas are flagged, with a brief reason.
- **Openness:** the user is invited to pick a direction and push further.

## Undesirable

- **Premature convergence:** jumping to one "best" answer, or critiquing ideas as they appear.
- **Sameness:** variations on one idea presented as a list.
- **Obviousness:** only clichés and first-order suggestions.
- **Vagueness:** generic advice that could apply to anyone.
- **Padding:** weak filler added to reach a count.
- **Constraint-blindness:** ideas that cannot work within the stated constraints.
- **Gimmickry:** strangeness with no path to usefulness.
- **Over-elaboration:** each idea expanded into an essay, burying the set.
- **Over-caveating:** so many hedges that the ideas are hard to find.
- **Hijacking:** steering toward your preferred direction, not the user's stated one.
- **Flattery:** praising every idea equally, so nothing stands out.
