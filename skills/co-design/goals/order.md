# Order

The work and its presentation follow an order that wastes nothing: first things first, related things together, and it is always clear where things stand.

*Falls short:* jumping around. *Overshoots:* procedure and structure for their own sake.

## Desirable

- **Logical sequence:** each step rests on what came before.
- **Dependency respect:** prerequisites are finished before the work that relies on them.
- **Prioritization:** the most important or most blocking items come first.
- **Clear structure:** material is in parts with an evident relationship.
- **Grouping:** related items sit together.
- **Hierarchy:** main points are distinguishable from supporting detail.
- **Consistency:** the same patterns, formats and naming are used throughout.
- **One thing at a time:** a thread is finished or parked on purpose before the next opens.
- **Signposting:** it is clear what is done, where things stand and what comes next.
- **Stable plan:** the sequence changes only when new information justifies it, and the change is stated.
- **Clean handoffs:** each stage ends in a state the next can pick up without rework.

## Undesirable

- **Out-of-sequence work:** steps are done before their prerequisites, forcing rework.
- **Jumping around:** attention moves between topics with no thread completed.
- **Buried lead:** the most important point is in the middle or at the end.
- **Jumble:** unrelated items are mixed with no organizing principle.
- **Flatness:** everything gets equal weight, so priorities can't be told apart.
- **Inconsistency:** formats, terms or conventions shift partway through.
- **Urgency over importance:** whatever arrived last is handled first, whatever the stated priorities.
- **Lost place:** nobody can tell what stage the work is in or what remains.
- **Rigid procedure:** a fixed sequence is followed when the situation calls for another.
- **Over-structuring:** simple content is wrapped in headers, numbering and layers it doesn't need.
- **Process over outcome:** effort goes into organizing the work instead of doing it.
- **Tidiness as disguise:** neat structure makes thin or wrong content look sound.
